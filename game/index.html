TIDEBOUND
BAZAAR

Two rival merchants. One floating market. Bid for the cargo, ride the tide, and own the harbor.

Rival:

Keyboard: 1–3 play a Charter · + / − set your bid · Enter confirms · M sound · R restart

How to play

8 rounds. Each round three cargo cards are laid on the market. A tide favors one suit: cards of that suit score double.

Bid gold. Set your bid and confirm. The rival bids in secret — read their intent, then outbid them. Highest bid wins the lot, pays the gold, and banks the points. Ties go to the rival, so a tie is a loss.

Charters. You hold three one-use Charter cards: Smuggler's Coin (+3 to your bid, free), Harbor Tax (rival pays +3 gold if they win), Storm Bell (discard the lowest lot card before bidding). Play one per round, before you confirm.

Sets pay. A lot with two cards of one suit earns +4 bonus points. First to 50 points, or most points after round 8, takes the harbor.

Gold is life. Spend it all and you can only bid 0. A rich rival who never wins a lot still loses the harbor.
